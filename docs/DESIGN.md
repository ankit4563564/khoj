# KHOJ visual reference

Reference: design-concept.png, generated with the built-in Image Gen tool.

Direction: graphite and muted green surfaces, coral accent, editorial serif headings, neutral sans-serif controls. Sidebar 240 px; fluid main workspace. Primary hierarchy: heading, candidate review panel, belongings, privacy reminder. No marketing wrapper.

Tokens: background #111514, surface #1b211f, border #343c37, text #f3f1e9, secondary #a7b1ab, accent #ff805f. Libre Caslon Display with Georgia fallback; DM Sans with Arial fallback. Buttons 7 px radius, panels 8 px. Outline icons from Lucide. Short fade/slide transitions; reduced motion respected.

Overview copy: KHOJ; Campus lost & found; Overview; My items; Unclaimed board; Report a find; Found something?; A little lost. Not gone.; Your belongings. Your campus. A way back.; POTENTIAL MATCH; Something familiar turned up.; A possible match for your AirPods Pro is ready to review.; Review potential match; 92%; Visual similarity; Your belongings; Register an item; AirPods Pro; Everyday bottle; Campus backpack; View item; Your details stay yours.; Identifying details are only shared after ownership is verified.

Intentional functional additions: demo review and recovery navigation, local storage disclosure, sample match disclaimer, footer. These make the prototype's scope and downstream flows explicit. Generated product triptych is displayed as three separate background positions; images are not screenshots of UI. UI remains semantic HTML. Public reports use a deliberately withheld-photo treatment instead of exposing identifiable photographs. Downstream forms and review states extend the same tokens.

Asset prompt: three equal photographic panels containing white earbuds, sage bottle, black backpack; charcoal desk and backdrop, natural soft light, no text or logos. The follow-up asset prompt kept the composition and moved the subjects into the middle 55% of panel height to make responsive cropping safe. Built-in Image Gen used; public/belongings-v2.png is the active project-owned asset.
